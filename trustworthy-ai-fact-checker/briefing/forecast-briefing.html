How this page works

  • One idea per card. Use Next and Back.
  • Every card has a box. Most are optional. Cards marked Required keep Next locked until you've written a real answer (about 15 characters or more).
  • Your answers save automatically. Claude reads them next session and replies to each one.
  • Select any text on a card and tap Ask about this to quote it into a question here.
